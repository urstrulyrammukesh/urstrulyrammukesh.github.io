About me

I'm Ram Mukesh Yadav, a B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Srinagar.

I like building things that solve real problems. I'm the founder and developer of Clinora, a health platform where patients find and book real doctors, have video visits, get prescriptions and medicine reminders, keep encrypted health records, and chat on WhatsApp, in 139 languages.

My goal is cybersecurity. I'm learning networking, Linux and web security, practising on CTF platforms, and documenting every step in public so others can learn with me.
