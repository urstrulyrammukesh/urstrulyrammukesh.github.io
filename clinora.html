Building Clinora: a health platform from zero to live

Clinora helps patients find and book real doctors, have video visits, get prescriptions and reminders, and keep their health records encrypted, in 139 languages. This page explains what I built, how it works and how I secured it.

02The problem

Booking a doctor usually means phone calls, long queues and paper prescriptions that get lost. Reminders are easy to miss, and health records end up scattered. Many patients are also more comfortable in their own language than in English.

I wanted one place where a patient can find a verified doctor, book and pay, visit in person or by video, and then get the prescription, reminders and records automatically, in their own language, on any phone.

03What it does

👩 For patients

  • Find verified doctors with filters and a smart map
  • Book and pay online, or join a clinic's live queue token
  • Video visits in the browser (WebRTC)
  • Printable prescriptions and medicine reminders by push, SMS and WhatsApp
  • Encrypted health records and family profiles

👨‍⚕️ For doctors & clinics

  • Doctor pages, verification and payouts
  • Queue tokens, clinic teams and dashboards
  • Patient history and receipts
  • Report and block for safety

🤖 AI features

  • AI receptionist chat
  • WhatsApp assistant
  • Prescription reading and translations

🗺️ Smart map

  • Exact GPS and follow-me compass map
  • Road routes and travel times (walk, cycle, car)
  • Live location sharing with a tracking link

05Security

1HTTPS everywhere with HSTS
2Strict Content-Security-Policy with a per-page nonce, Subresource Integrity on outside scripts
3Rate limits and CSRF checks
4HttpOnly, SameSite sessions; PBKDF2 passwords (310k rounds); email and phone codes
5Two-factor login (authenticator app + recovery codes)
6Roles (patient · doctor · admin), verified doctors, report and block
7AES-256-GCM encrypted records with daily checked backups

08What I learned

Security is a process

An audit on a "finished" app still found 9 issues. Automated scanners on every push catch problems before they go live.

Limit anything that costs money

The AI chat had no rate limit, so anyone could run up the bill. Every paid API now has limits.

Tests make big changes safe

45 end-to-end tests with fake payment, WhatsApp and storage services let me ship many features quickly without breaking old ones.

Keep the stack simple

A standard-library Python server and plain JavaScript kept the app fast, easy to host and easy to read.
